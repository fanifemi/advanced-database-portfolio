4. QUERIES AND AGGREGATION PIPELINES
==========================================================================================

  Q1  Customer-360 lookup by phone (support agent) — ONE read returns the whole profile
      found Adaeze Okafor with 2 account(s), 2 KYC doc(s), 1 device(s) in 2.51 ms
      (the same view in PostgreSQL joins 6 tables)

  Q2  Which customer owns account number X? (multikey index on accounts.account_number)
      1000000011 -> customer 1 Adaeze Okafor

  Q3  KYC funnel — documents by type and status ($unwind + $group)
      NIN             SUBMITTED       1
      NIN             VERIFIED        5
      PASSPORT        VERIFIED        1
      UTILITY_BILL    VERIFIED        1
      VOTERS_CARD     REJECTED        1

  Q4  Customers by KYC tier, with share that run a business ($group + $cond)
      tier 1:   1,490 customers,      0 with a business profile
      tier 2:     905 customers,      0 with a business profile
      tier 3:     605 customers,    203 with a business profile

  Q5  FRAUD SIGNAL — devices used by 3 or more different customers ($unwind + $group + $addToSet)
      dev-ring-040     shared by   6 customers e.g. [200007, 200507, 201007, 201507, 202007, 202507]
