   client saw: DETAIL:  The transaction has already committed locally, but might not have been replicated to the standby.
   client saw: 17754
=> With one synchronous standby, losing that standby blocks every commit: the primary chose CONSISTENCY over AVAILABILITY.

Fix: quorum commit — any 1 of the 2 replicas may acknowledge:
