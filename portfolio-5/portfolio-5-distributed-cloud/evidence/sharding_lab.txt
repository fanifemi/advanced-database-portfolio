S4  SCATTER-GATHER AGGREGATION — what can and cannot be pushed to the shards
======================================================================================
(a) Grouped BY the shard key: every group lives on one shard, so each shard computes final results in parallel
                                                   QUERY PLAN                                                   
 Limit
   ->  Sort
         Sort Key: (sum(wallet_ledger.amount)) DESC
         ->  Append
               ->  Async Foreign Scan
                     Relations: Aggregate on (public.wallet_ledger_s1 wallet_ledger)
                     Remote SQL: SELECT customer_id, count(*), sum(amount) FROM public.wallet_ledger GROUP BY 1
               ->  Async Foreign Scan
                     Relations: Aggregate on (public.wallet_ledger_s2 wallet_ledger_1)
                     Remote SQL: SELECT customer_id, count(*), sum(amount) FROM public.wallet_ledger GROUP BY 1

S5  CROSS-SHARD TRANSFER WITHOUT TWO-PHASE COMMIT — atomicity can break
======================================================================================
postgres_fdw commits each shard's sub-transaction one after another when the coordinator commits.
If a LATER shard fails at commit, the EARLIER shard has already committed and cannot be undone.
Both directions are tried; N60,000 pushes the receiver to N160,000, which its shard rejects at COMMIT.
